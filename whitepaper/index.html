1. Executive Summary

Burnaverse Protocol is a BNB Chain token ecosystem designed to make community participation more transparent, accessible and rewarding. The project uses $BUV as the core digital asset for participation, identity alignment, campaign access and future ecosystem utilities.

The concept is simple: combine culture, blockchain and community into one branded Web3 movement where holders can follow public milestones, verify the contract, join official channels and participate in future experiences as they are released.

Primary contract: 0xd14ec02a022d2bd4117a0eeba966423253a48ad1. Users should always verify this address before interacting with $BUV.

7. Liquidity & Market Access

Burnaverse Protocol is designed for BUV/BNB market access through decentralized exchanges on BNB Chain. The preferred early-stage structure is a simple BUV/BNB V2 liquidity pair because it is easier for new users to understand and keeps liquidity active across the full price curve.

Liquidity size strongly affects price stability. Very small liquidity pools can experience high price movement from small buys or sells. Users should understand slippage, pool depth and volatility before trading.

Liquidity policy recommendations

  • Publish the official pool link after liquidity is added.
  • Publish liquidity-lock or burn proof if LP tokens are locked or burned.
  • Avoid changing official trading links without public notice.
  • Maintain clear contract address visibility across the website and social profiles.

9. Governance Framework

Governance should not be rushed. Burnaverse Protocol may introduce governance gradually after the project establishes stable communication, active community participation and clear operational processes.

  • Proposal submission rules and minimum eligibility requirements.

  • Transparent execution process for approved proposals.
  • Treasury controls such as multi-signature authorization where appropriate.
